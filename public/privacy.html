TILTFALL · PRIVACY POLICY

Effective 8 September 2026. Applies to the game at tiltfall.netlify.app and the Telegram Mini App @tiltfall_bot.

What we store

Playing in Telegram. When you open the game inside Telegram, Telegram hands us your numeric user id, first name and username, signed by Telegram. We store the id, the display name, your level, the skin you wear, your best and lifetime scores, the number of runs, and the time of your last run — that is what the leaderboard shows. A run that would enter the leaderboard is submitted with a compact replay of your inputs so the server can re-run it and confirm the score; replays are checked and not retained.

Progress. Coins, boosters, level, skins and daily tasks are saved in your browser's local storage. In Telegram they are also mirrored to Telegram CloudStorage, which is tied to your Telegram account and lets a second device pick up where you left off.

Purchases. Payments are made with Telegram Stars through Telegram's own payment sheet. We never see card or wallet details. We keep the Telegram payment charge id together with what it bought for twelve months, so a refund can be matched to the item it returns.

Invites. If you open the game through a friend's invite link, we store the friend's user id next to yours, and whether the invite bonus has been paid.

Counters. Every open increments an aggregate counter for the link it came from (for example "itch", "forum", or the host of the referring page). These counters contain no personal data.

What we do not do

  • No cookies, no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No account or e-mail required — the web version needs nothing at all.

Where it lives

Leaderboard, purchase and invite records are held in a Redis database operated by Upstash in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). The site and API run on Netlify. Telegram CloudStorage is operated by Telegram under its own terms.

Deleting your data

RESET PROGRESS inside the game erases your local progress, your CloudStorage mirror and your leaderboard entry. For anything else — purchase records, invite records — message us through the channel below and we will remove it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. Questions: t.me/tiltfall_news.
